AI + Full-Stack Project

ExamFlux

AI-Powered Exam Authoring and Review Platform is the product name for this system: users provide a prompt or upload a .docx file, then GenAI drafts structured exam items. Each item is checked for validity, compared against existing content with semantic embeddings, and sent through an approval workflow before it is committed to the database.

Project Overview

2025 ETS Spot Award

Developed a reusable prompt-based LLM workflow for complex TOEIC multi-passage item generation by decomposing inference and synthesis tasks into structured generation steps. The approach addressed a projected $56K+ in annual outsourced item-writer costs and earned an ETS Spot Award (2025).

ExamFlux was inspired by that work: I built a system that turns prompt engineering into a structured workflow for generating higher-order exam items, such as synthesis and inference questions, while preserving human review and quality control.

The Challenge

Manual exam-item creation is slow, inconsistent, and difficult to scale. GenAI can accelerate production, but without strong system-level controls it often generates weak, repetitive, or structurally invalid items. I needed a technical workflow that could combine generation speed with validation, consistency, and reviewer accountability.

My Approach

I built a full-stack architecture that combines structured generation, schema validation, semantic duplicate detection, and a human approval workflow. The system uses constrained prompts as part of a larger engineering pipeline, with backend validation and review checkpoints to ensure generated exam items are high quality, traceable, and production-ready.

Key Features

ExamFlux brings together generation, validation, similarity analysis, and human review in one AI-assisted exam authoring workflow.

Prompt-to-Item Generation

Users provide a prompt or upload .docx content, and GenAI generates draft exam items in a structured format ready for review.

Validation Guardrails

Each generated item is checked against formatting and content constraints so invalid choices and malformed output are filtered out early.

Duplicate Detection

Embedding-based similarity checks help identify near-duplicate question items before they reach the final inventory.

Human Review Workflow

Reviewers can approve, reject, or commit items, ensuring AI-generated content remains transparent and accountable.
