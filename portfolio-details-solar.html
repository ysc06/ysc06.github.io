New Jersey Solar Journey: A Narrative Visualization of Solar Policy

A five-scene D3.js story that plots New Jersey’s solar goals against actual installations, one policy era per scene.

Project
Interactive Narrative Visualization
Context
UIUC CS 416 — Data Visualization
Role
Sole author — data preparation, narrative design, D3 implementation
Stack
D3.js v7, d3-annotation, JavaScript, HTML/CSS

Project Overview

The story the data tells

The rise: from 23 kW of installed capacity in 2001 to 4.4 GW by 2023 — New Jersey spent most of two decades ahead of its own published targets, holding #4 nationally through the SREC years.

The turn: in 2023 the state fell out of the national Top 10 for the first time in twenty years — just as its new target demands 17 GW by 2035, roughly 1.05 GW every year, about triple the pace of the decade behind it.

What I Built

Built for UIUC CS 416, Data Visualization. I derived annual and cumulative capacity from national installation records, matched it to the state’s published goals, and interpolated toward the 17 GW target for 2035. Each scene covers one policy era, from the Clean Energy Program to the 2035 Energy Master Plan, with actual installations drawn over the dashed goal line, the lead shaded in green, and d3-annotation labels at each milestone. One scene state controls the year range, annotation and growth rate, and readers can click to pin exact values in any scene.
